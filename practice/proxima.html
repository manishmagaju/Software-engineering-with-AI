Proxima International Academy (PIA) is a progressive co-educational school established and operated by a highly qualified, dedicated and motivated team of school leaders. It offers life and soft skill based learning and behavior modification opportunities to each student to develop their academic potentials and meaningful life.

VISION

Proxima International Academy aspires to be a premium school to nurture student's individual strengths, talents, intelligences, and transition them to be a globally competent and responsible citizen.

MISSION

Proxima International Academy provides opportunities to students to explore, identify and enhance their own innate potentials and strengths for their holistic development through a blend of latest tools, techniques, philosophies and pedagogies supported by skills and highly qualified intellectual resources.
